Our Foundation

Built by community, for community.

The Dee Will Smith Foundation (DWS Foundation) is a Liberia-based humanitarian foundation, founded in 2017 and currently pursuing nonprofit registration in the United States to broaden its reach and support. Our work is grounded in a simple belief: Liberia's future is best built by investing directly in its people.

We partner with families, schools, and local leaders across Liberia to design programs that respond to what communities actually need — from classroom support and vocational training to protection services for children and start-up support for young entrepreneurs.

Our mission is to expand opportunity for Liberia's youth, women, children, and young adults through education, skills training, health support, and community-led programs that help people build the future they choose for themselves.

Dignity

People first, always

Every program is designed to respect the dignity and agency of the people we serve, not to speak for them.

Community

Local leadership

We work through Liberian communities and local partners, not around them, so support fits real, local needs.

Opportunity

Tools that last

We favor education, training, and support that build lasting capability, not one-time relief alone.

Integrity

Accountable by design

As a foundation entrusted with community support, we are committed to transparency in how resources are used.

Where We Focus

Four groups. One shared future.

Our programs are organized around the people we exist to serve — each shaped by direct conversations with the communities we work in.

Youth

Youth Empowerment

Education access, tutoring support, mentorship, and leadership development that help young people stay in school and grow into confident leaders.

Women

Women's Development

Vocational training, small-business support, and health education that help women build independent, sustainable livelihoods.

Children

Child Welfare

School supplies, nutrition support, and protection services that give children a safer, more stable path through early life.

Young Adults

Young Adult Opportunity

Job-readiness training, apprenticeship placement, and support for first-time entrepreneurs building their own income.

How We Work

A steady process, not a one-time gesture.

Humanitarian work only holds up when it is done with communities, in the right order, over time.

01 — Listen

Start with the community

We begin every program by listening to the families, schools, and local leaders who understand the need firsthand.

02 — Partner

Work through local partners

We build programs alongside Liberian organizations, schools, and volunteers rather than operating apart from them.

03 — Invest

Put resources into people

Funding goes toward direct support — education, training, care, and start-up resources — with minimal overhead.

04 — Follow through

Stay accountable

We check back with the people and communities we support, and adjust our programs based on what's actually working.
